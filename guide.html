How to Plot Coordinates & Draw Ranges on a Map

A complete guide to using the Coordinate Range Mapper — from entering DMS coordinates to calculating polygon area. No registration required.

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Enter Your Coordinates

You can input coordinates in three formats:

  • DMS (Degrees-Minutes-Seconds style): 40-42.50N for latitude, 074-00.40W for longitude
  • Decimal: 40.70833, -74.00667
  • DMM (Degrees Decimal Minutes): 40°42.50'N, 074°00.40'W

Type into the Longitude and Latitude fields, then click Add. Or click directly on the map to drop a point.

2

Batch Add Multiple Points

For multiple coordinates, expand the Batch Input section and paste them all at once — one pair per line. The tool auto-detects the format:

40-42.50N, 074-00.40W
51-30.20N, 000-07.50W
35-41.30N, 139-41.50E

Click Batch Add to append, or Replace All to start fresh.

3

Close the Polygon & View Results

Once you have 3 or more points, click Close Polygon. The tool:

  • Connects the last point back to the first, forming a closed shape
  • Highlights the range area in green on the map
  • Displays the area (km² or m²) and perimeter (km or m)
4

Convert or Export

Need to convert your coordinates to a different format? Use the Coordinate Converter to switch between DMS, Decimal, and DMM.

Want to save your polygon? Click Export GeoJSON to download a standard GeoJSON file compatible with QGIS, Google Earth, and other GIS tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

What coordinate formats does the mapper support?
DMS (40-42.50N), Decimal (40.7083), and DMM (40°42.50'N). Batch input accepts mixed formats with comma, semicolon, space, or tab delimiters.
How do I calculate the area of a polygon?
Add at least 3 points, then click Close Polygon. The area (in km² or m²) and perimeter appear automatically below the coordinate list.
Can I export my data?
Yes. After closing the polygon, use the Export button to download a GeoJSON file. It includes all your points and the polygon geometry.
Is this tool really free?
Completely. No registration, no API key, no data collection. Built with open-source Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. The source code is on GitHub.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The interface is fully responsive — the panel stacks above the map on smaller screens. You can tap the map to add points, just like on desktop.
